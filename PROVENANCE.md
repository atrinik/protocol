# Provenance and clean-room boundary

Game Protocol 1 schemas, specifications, fixtures, tests, and infrastructure
are independently authored MIT work. Public product decisions, independently
licensed data, black-box behavior, and interoperability facts may inform the
contract. GPL classic schemas, command tables, generated bindings, source,
tests, and structure-preserving translations may not be used as implementation
inputs.

An approved historical MIT grant may be used only under the exhaustive
registry and proof requirements in the pinned `atrinik/atrinik` root policy.
Complete non-shallow history, including renames, must prove sole original
authorship by the named grantor and exclude embedded third-party or conflicting
material. Current blame, committer identity, recollection, or repository-level
ownership is insufficient. Mixed or uncertain evidence is excluded.

Every candidate is recorded in [`provenance/reuse.json`](provenance/reuse.json)
with immutable source, history/identity evidence, embedded-material review,
destination, transformation, copyright, grant, and reviewer. Generated files
are admitted only as mechanical outputs of the repository's MIT schemas and
pinned permissive generators. Content and assets remain external data under
their actual licenses; describing a digest or ID never relicenses them.
